Apollo M-1 · 64 × 64

Arcade

A physical LED matrix on the wall that plays real games — Snake and Tetris you can control, self-playing demos as ambient art, and a live video mirror. It's driven from a little service running on the home network.

Control Panel

Opens the live controller — pick a game, steer with your keyboard or the on-screen pad, and watch it on the panel. Works when you're on the home network.

Open Control Panel

Snake

Classic Snake, crisp at 32×32. Arrow keys or WASD.

Tetris

Full Tetris well with rotation, line clears and hard drop.

Self-play (ambient)

The games play themselves as living wall art when idle.

Video mirror

Mirror any window — emulators, cartoons — downscaled to the panel.
