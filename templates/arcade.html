Apollo M-1 · 64 × 64

Arcade

A physical LED matrix on the wall that plays real games — Snake, Tetris, Pong, Breakout, Tron, Flappy, Invaders, Dodge, 2048 and Life — each with a self-playing auto mode for ambient wall art, plus video mirror, file loop and phone cast. Driven from a service on the home network.

Control Panel

The full desktop dashboard — pick a game, steer with your keyboard, tweak backgrounds and mirror settings. Works on the home network.

Phone Remote

Hold it sideways like a game controller. Browse and pick games right on the panel — the phone is just the D-pad and buttons. Home WiFi only.
